How an evening runs

Four beats.

  1. She arrives about ninety minutes early.

    Ingredients on the counter, knives out, pots on the stove. If you want to hang out and watch her set up, she likes company.

  2. Guests sit at the counter or the table.

    Whichever puts them closest. She wants to be able to hand plates directly. Wine glasses full. Music low.

  3. Five courses come out over three hours.

    Soup, salad or an appetizer, a fish course, the main, and dessert. She talks while she cooks. Where curried goat comes from, why the pot has to be heavy, what her grandmother did differently.

  4. She cleans the kitchen.

    You do not touch a dish. She leaves it the way she found it.
