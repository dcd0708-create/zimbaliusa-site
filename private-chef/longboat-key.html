Why a Jamaican chef

The food most private chefs on the coast don’t know how to cook.

Longboat Key has plenty of private chefs. Most of them cook Mediterranean, or steakhouse, or coastal-American. Chef Alecia cooks curried goat that takes four hours, jerk with the char it is supposed to have, escovitch snapper under pickled peppers. She has done this every night for twenty years at Zimbali Retreats in Negril.

She can also cook a Gulf snapper straight, or a filet, or a roast chicken with the pan sauce. But most people book her because they want food they cannot easily get anywhere else on the coast.

How it works

Five steps, none of them yours.

  2. We build a menu.

  5. She cooks, serves, and cleans.

    You sit at the table. She handles the kitchen from arrival to departure.
