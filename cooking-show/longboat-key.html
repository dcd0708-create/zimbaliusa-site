The Live Cooking Show · Longboat Key

A live Jamaican cooking show in your Longboat Key kitchen.

Better than a private chef dinner. Chef Alecia cooks in front of your guests.

She drives out from Sarasota, arrives with the ingredients, and lays them out where everyone can see them — the scotch bonnets, the ginger, the green bananas, the fish. Then she cooks in front of you, and she talks while she does it. Five courses over about three hours. Longboat Key is well inside her free travel radius; roughly a 30 minutes drive.

Longboat has the space for a real dinner party. The estates on the bay side, the beach houses on Gulf of Mexico Drive — most of them have open kitchens that hold twelve or fifteen at a counter without anyone feeling crowded. Good acoustics for the way she cooks, which is out loud.

For twenty years, this was the reason travelers drove into the hills outside Negril instead of eating on the beach. Now you don’t have to.

How the evening unfolds

About three hours from first course to last.

  1. Setup, 30 minutes before

    She arrives with the ingredients and lays them out on the counter. Guests can watch or hold a drink somewhere else — up to them.

  2. First course, 6:30

    An amuse-bouche, cooked and plated in front of the room.

  3. Courses two through four

    Soup, salad, and entrée. She talks through what she’s doing while she does it. Questions get answered in real time.

  4. Dessert, around 9:00

    Usually the fruit flambé. Sometimes something else, depending on what came in at the farm market.

  5. Cleanup

    She stays late enough that the kitchen is put back the way she found it.

Frequently asked

The questions people actually ask.

What does the price include?

All the food, all the juices, all the prep, all the cleanup. Alecia arrives with the ingredients and leaves with the trash. You provide the kitchen and the people.

How many guests?

Two guests or thirty — the $1,200 minimum booking is the same either way. Parties over twelve get a kitchen assistant included.

How long does it take?

Plan on about three hours from first course to last, plus a half hour on either side for setup and breakdown.

Do I need a special kitchen in my Longboat Key rental?

A stove, a sink, oven, and counter space is enough. Most Longboat Key kitchens work. She brings her own knives and pots.

Can she handle dietary restrictions?

Yes. Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, nut allergies, seafood allergies — she has cooked around all of them for twenty years. Tell us on the inquiry and the menu comes back adjusted.

How do I book?

Send an inquiry with your date, guest count, and Longboat Key address. You will get a menu and quote back within one business day. A 30 percent deposit holds the date; the balance is due 48 hours before the event. PayPal (card or account) or Zelle.
