Dietary needs

Gluten-free, nut-free, and severe allergies.

Tell us on the inquiry and the menu comes back adjusted. Gluten-free swaps are straightforward — most Jamaican cooking is naturally gluten-free once you take out the festival and the dumplings, and we can offer alternatives that fit the rest of the meal.

Nut allergies, seafood allergies, and kosher-adjacent needs: name them clearly on the inquiry. Alecia has cooked around all of it for twenty years without a separate plate.

The juices

Pressed the afternoon of the dinner.

  • Soursop

    Creamy, cold, floral. Nobody has this in a bottle. Everyone asks for a second glass.

  • June plum

    Tart, tropical, cuts the richness of the mains.

  • Sorrel

    Hibiscus flowers steeped with ginger and clove. Served cold. It is the color of a good garnet.

  • Tamarind

    Puckery, deep. Alecia sweetens it with brown sugar and lime.

  • Cucumber-ginger

    Green, sharp, refreshing. The one non-drinkers finish first.

  • Coconut water

    Straight from the coconut when we can source them locally.
