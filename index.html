The food

Jamaican cooking, done properly, without the shortcuts.

Jerk chicken with the char it’s supposed to have. Coconut curried shrimp. Escovitch snapper under pickled onion and pepper. Crispy plantain cakes. Stir-fried vegetables. Roasted breadfruit and yam. Coconut cream pumpkin soup. Conch and Florida lobster when the season allows.

And juice — soursop, June plum, sorrel, tamarind, cucumber and ginger — pressed that afternoon, not poured from a bottle.

Vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free and allergy guests are welcome and well taken care of. Five courses of their own, cooked with the same care as everyone else’s. She did this for two decades for tables of thirty, and it is not an afterthought.

* Curried goat by request, at additional cost. It takes four hours and it’s worth it.

Who she is

She learned this in the hills, from her grandparents.

Chef Alecia grew up on a family farm in the Jamaican hills, raised by her grandparents, cooking over woodfire before she cooked over anything else.

In 2005 she founded Zimbali Retreats in Negril. Its kitchen was ranked #1 in Jamaica on Tripadvisor for ten straight years. Hurricane Melissa ended the Jamaica chapter in October 2025. She landed in Sarasota with the same knives and the same recipes.
